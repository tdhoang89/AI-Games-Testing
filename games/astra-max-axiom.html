The art of the fold

A little closer.

  1. Draw between the twins. Hold and drag a seam across their connecting line, roughly at a right angle.
  2. Watch for the glow. Both lights glow when they fit. Release to unite them. A centered fold earns a precision bonus.
  3. Find a shared seam. Unite several pairs in one fold for much bigger points. Consecutive successful folds build flow up to ×5.
  4. Keep your three threads. A faded pair costs one. Coral anchors inside your glowing strip tear a fold and cost one, too.
